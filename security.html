    The questions procurement asks, answered before they ask.

    What we hold, what we do not hold yet, and the documents we will send. A young company should be precise about the difference.

    In short

    Client data is processed under a DPA, encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is role-based. Client material is not used to train shared or foundation models. Quantocos is not yet ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified; the controls are documented and available for review, and we will publish the certificates when they exist.

    What we will send

    The pack, not a promise

    Ask for the security pack and you get the documents a review actually needs: the DPA, the subprocessor list, the AI assurance note and the retention position.

    We will also tell you what is missing. If a control you require is not in place, the useful answer is no — not a slide that implies yes.

    Data and AI

    What happens to the material you send

    Client files are used to do the work, not to improve a shared model. Access is limited to the pod and the specialists assigned to the engagement.

    Tenancy

    One client, one boundary

    Briefs, assets and review notes are held against the client account. Access is role-based, and a specialist outside the pod does not inherit it.

    Models

    No shared-model training

    Client material is not used to train shared or foundation models. Where a client wants a private model, it is scoped and priced separately.

    Retention

    Defined in the DPA

    The DPA states what is kept, for how long and how deletion is handled at the end of the engagement.

    Agents

    A human signs the output

    Agents draft and repeat; a named specialist approves. The approval is recorded, not assumed.

    Questions

    Procurement questions, answered

    Is Quantocos ISO 27001 certified?

    Not yet. The company was incorporated in 2026 and does not hold an ISO 27001 certificate or a SOC 2 report. The control set is documented and available for review; we will publish the certificate when it exists rather than imply it today.

    Where is client data processed?

    Primary delivery is from India. Data residency is assessed per engagement, and any deviation is written into the DPA. We do not claim a local data center in every buyer market.

    Do you train AI models on client data?

    No. Client material is not used to train shared or foundation models. Access is controlled per client, and the terms are written into the DPA.

    Can we review your security pack before signing?

    Yes. Ask for the security pack through the contact form and we will send the DPA, the current subprocessor list and the AI assurance note.

    Who signs off on an AI-assisted deliverable?

    A named human specialist. Agents draft and repeat; a specialist reviews and approves before anything is released, and the approval is recorded.
