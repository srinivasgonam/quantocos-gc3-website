    A launch has a date that cannot move.

    Every asset — film, renders, the microsite, the broker pack — has to be ready on the same morning.

    The pod we would put on it

    REMAU

    Scoped per client · 240 hours a month typical, 280 at peak

    Compare all six pods

    What the pod does

    Three things it takes off your desk

    • Launch campaigns: film, 3D, XR walkthroughs and the microsite that holds them
    • Broker and investor packs produced to a single template
    • Always-on content for the units that did not sell in the first wave

    The objection

    Can you hold a launch date?

    REMAU runs a minimum of three months precisely so the peak weeks are staffed in advance rather than absorbed at the last minute.

    1 PM, creative, digital, 3D and XR specialists, plus 5 AI agents.

    The constraint is a date that cannot move. Peak weeks are staffed in advance, and the broker pack, campaign and microsite share one calendar.

    How a month runs

    Launch weeks are staffed backwards

    • Week 1: launch date, asset list and approval chain fixed in one calendar
    • Weeks 2-6: film, 3D, microsite and broker pack produced in parallel
    • Weeks 7-10: paid launch, enquiry routing and sales enablement go live
    • After launch: always-on content for unsold units and the next phase

    What is never charged

    No add-ons, no surprise line items

    Onboarding and brand ingestion, brief clarification, asset handover and the monthly review are included. Software, licenses, AI generation, stock, fonts and pod management sit inside the subscription.

    No per-revision charge. No per-asset charge. No onboarding fee.

    Representative engagement — illustrative scope, not a client result

    A launch in fourteen weeks

    A residential phase with a fixed announcement date. The pod runs the campaign, the 3D, the microsite and the enquiry routing on one calendar, so the broker pack and the paid launch do not arrive in different weeks.

    Weeks 1–2Brand and project ingestion, launch calendar, asset list and the broker-pack template signed off once.
    Weeks 3–6Launch film and 3D walkthrough produced; microsite copy and build; NRI campaign variants.
    Weeks 7–10Broker enablement, paid launch, enquiry routing into the CRM and the first response review.
    Weeks 11–14Always-on content for unsold units, the monthly ledger and the next-phase calendar.
    What ships
    • Launch film and 3D walkthrough
    • Microsite and broker pack
    • NRI campaign set
    • CRM and enquiry routing

    The dates and deliverables are a representative scope. They are not a client outcome and no result is claimed.
