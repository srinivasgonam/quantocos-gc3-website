    Credits & pricing basis

    You always know what a piece of work costs before it starts

    Every request in the portal is estimated in credits before a pod picks it up. That estimate is visible to you, the spend is visible to you, and the closing balance is visible to you. There is no reconciliation meeting.

    01Credits, not timesheetsOne unit of work, estimated before it starts and visible in the portal.→
    02Fixed monthly priceA subscription and a credit pool, not an estimate that becomes an argument at the end of the month.→
    03Everything includedSoftware, licenses, AI generation, stock, fonts and pod management sit inside the fee.→
    04Additive tiersDigital inherits Creative, Strategy inherits both. The credit pool is spendable across everything you inherit.→
    Never charged to your credits

    The five items that cost you nothing

    • Onboarding and brand ingestion
    • Brief clarification and scoping conversation
    • Asset handover and file delivery
    • The monthly pod review
    • Credit estimates and cost transparency in the portal

    These are overhead we carry, not work we bill. They exist so the pod starts producing rather than administrating.

    Delivery today

    Human-delivered, with agentic tooling. Several tools in the stack — from Prism's surfaces to QREK — are in active development; the pods currently run on the parts that are proven, and clients are told which is which.
