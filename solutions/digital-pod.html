    The month, drawn

    Digital Pod, as a month of credits

    In short

    The Digital Pod is a monthly subscription team from Quantocos priced at $6,000/month for 240 credits. It combines the Creative and digital marketing catalogs in one pod of a manager, four human specialists and four AI agents, covering content production, SEO, answer-engine optimization, paid media, landing pages and analytics.

    What it replaces

    The hire or the supplier this stands in for

    A digital marketing agency plus a creative team, as a single function.

    Splitting creative and performance across two suppliers is the most common way a marketing budget produces nothing measurable. The agency that runs the ads blames the creative; the studio that makes the creative never sees the campaign data; and nobody is accountable for the month as a whole.

    The credit maths

    How 240 credits actually get spent

    Two hundred and forty credits split roughly in half. Production — twelve posts, four videos, a landing page — takes about a hundred and twenty. Performance — SEO, answer-engine work, paid media management, analytics and lifecycle — takes the other hundred and twenty. The split moves as the quarter demands.

    Buying questions

    What to settle before you subscribe

    The Digital Pod costs $6,000/month and includes 240 credits, which is twice the Creative Pod's pool. It contains the Creative and digital marketing catalogs, so credits can be spent on either production or performance work.

    The Creative Pod is production only: content, design, copy and video. The Digital Pod adds digital marketing — SEO, answer-engine optimization, paid media, landing pages, analytics and lifecycle email — and doubles the team and the credit pool.

    For most mid-market businesses, yes: it replaces the creative retainer and the media retainer with one pod, one manager and one dashboard.

    AEO and GEO work is the practice of making a business legible to answer engines and generative engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — so it is quoted or cited when someone asks a question in that market. The Digital Pod includes it alongside conventional SEO.

    Yes. The pods are additive: the Digital Pod includes Creative, and the Strategy Pod includes both. Most teams start with one pod, run a quarter, then move up or add a custom pod alongside it.

    The pod works across US, UK, EU, UAE and ANZ time zones. Billing is in US dollars.
