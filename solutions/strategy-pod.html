    In short

    The Strategy Pod, also called the Growth Pod, is Quantocos's top subscription tier at $9,000/month for 360 credits. One manager, six human specialists and six AI agents cover brand and go-to-market strategy, positioning, research, pricing, and the creative and digital marketing execution that follows.

    What it replaces

    The hire or the supplier this stands in for

    A fractional CMO or CTO, plus a digital agency, plus a creative team — as one function, priced below the retainer that covers only the first of the three.

    Strategy and execution are sold by different people, which is why so much strategy is never implemented. A fractional CMO produces a positioning document; a media agency produces campaign data; the two rarely share an author, a calendar or a definition of success.

    How the month runs

    Four weeks, then it repeats

    01Weeks 1–2Positioning, research and pricing, written in the open
    02Weeks 3–4The same pod executes the first campaign against that positioning

    04QuarterlyFull strategy reset: what the market did, what we assumed, what changes
    The credit maths

    How 360 credits actually get spent

    Three hundred and sixty credits is about one third strategy and two thirds execution: roughly a hundred and twenty on positioning, research and pricing, and two hundred and forty on the creative and digital work that follows from it. A client who wants strategy only can hold the credits there and spend nothing on production.

    What to settle before you subscribe

    The Strategy Pod costs $9,000/month and includes 360 credits. It contains the strategy, creative and digital marketing catalogs, so the same pod that writes the positioning also produces the campaign that follows it.

    A fractional CMO retainer typically runs $10,000–$12,000/month, with most engagements between $8,000 and $15,000, and buys senior thinking without a production team. The Strategy Pod costs $9,000 and includes strategy, creative and digital execution.
