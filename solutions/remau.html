    In short

    REMAU is Quantocos's real-estate marketing and automation pod, scoped per client, with 240 hours a month as the usual starting scope with up to 280 hours in peak weeks. It covers project launch campaigns, NRI-targeted digital marketing for the US, UK, UAE and ANZ diaspora, 3D walkthroughs and XR visualisation, CRM and lead-funnel automation, and RERA-aware marketing.

    What it replaces

    The hire or the supplier this stands in for

    A real-estate marketing agency, a property CGI studio and a CRM consultant, on one launch calendar.

    A property launch has a date that cannot move. Renders, walkthroughs, diaspora demand campaigns and the CRM that catches the enquiries all have to arrive together — and they are usually bought from four different suppliers, each with their own timeline and their own idea of what the launch is.

    The credit maths

    How a scoped engagement is estimated

    Two hundred and forty hours against a launch: roughly sixty on campaign strategy and creative, eighty on digital and diaspora media, seventy on 3D and XR production, and thirty on CRM and pipeline automation. Peak weeks draw up to two hundred and eighty from the shared pool without renegotiating the contract.

    Buying questions

    What to settle before you subscribe

    REMAU is Quantocos's real-estate marketing and automation pod. It combines project launch marketing, NRI-targeted digital campaigns, 3D and XR visualisation, CRM automation and RERA-aware communication in one team — scoped per client, with 240 hours a month as the usual starting scope.

    REMAU is scoped per client, with 240 included hours a month in the usual scope and capacity for 280 hours during two surge weeks a year. The minimum engagement is three months because of the depth of setup on a launch.

    REMAU runs on the shared pod model: a dedicated pod manager for your account and access to a shared specialist pool. That is what allows peak weeks to draw 280 hours from a 240-hour subscription without renegotiating the contract.

    NRI-targeted campaigns run across the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates and Australia and New Zealand, in English.
