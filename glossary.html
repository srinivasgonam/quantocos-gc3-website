    Glossary

    The words buyers ask us to define, in plain language.

    Global capability center, pod, credit, human-in-the-loop, AEO, GEO and the rest of the vocabulary used on this site.

    In short

    A global capability center is a team in another market that performs a function for the buyer. A pod is a monthly subscription for that function, containing a manager, specialists, agents and a credit pool. A credit is the unit the pod bills in. Human-in-the-loop means a named specialist approves every AI-assisted output before release.

    Global capability center (GCC)
    A captive or contracted team in a lower-cost location that performs a function for a business in another market. A GCC is usually owned or directly employed by the buyer, which carries the recruitment, management and utilisation risk. GCA3 is the branded capability center operated by Quantocos, sold as pods rather than as a captive build.
    Pod
    A monthly subscription for a function, not a project. A pod contains a named manager, human specialists, AI agents and the tools they need, and draws on a defined pool of credits. The client approves output; the pod owns the calendar, the production and the review gate.
    Credit
    The unit a pod bills in. Each catalog item has a credit estimate agreed before work starts, and the monthly allowance is a pool rather than a per-person timesheet. Credits do not roll over at the end of the month.
    Human-in-the-loop
    A delivery model in which an agent drafts or repeats work and a named human specialist reviews and approves it before release. It is a workflow, not a disclaimer: the approval is recorded and can be audited.
    Agent
    Software that performs a bounded task — drafting, resizing, transcription, reporting or first-pass generation — under a defined brief. In a pod, agents handle repeatable work; specialists handle judgment, craft and sign-off.
    Credit ledger
    The portal view of what has been spent and what remains in the monthly credit pool. It shows the brief, the credit estimate, the status and the month's balance, so the invoice does not arrive as a surprise.
    Answer engine optimization (AEO)
    The practice of structuring a page so an answer engine can extract and cite a direct answer: a short answer block under the heading, question-shaped sections, a facts table and structured data. It is related to SEO but optimises for citation rather than a ranked link.
    Generative engine optimization (GEO)
    The practice of making a brand and its facts easy for a generative model to quote correctly: stable entity identifiers, a consistent description, explicit authorship and a machine-readable record. It is not the same as ranking in a search result.
    REMAU
    The real-estate pod. It combines project marketing, XR and 3D visualisation, diaspora demand campaigns and pipeline automation on a launch clock. It is a minimum three-month engagement because the peak weeks are staffed in advance.
    Data processing agreement (DPA)
    The contract that states what personal data is processed, why, where, for how long and by which subprocessors. Quantocos provides a DPA for review before an engagement starts.
    Subprocessor
    A third party that processes client data on behalf of the delivery team — for example a cloud host or an AI provider. Subprocessors are named in the DPA and the current list is sent with the security pack.
    Why the definitions matter

    The words are the contract

    Most of the confusion in a capability-center purchase comes from three words used loosely: team, project and subscription. A pod is the third one, and the commercial terms follow from that.

    If a definition here does not match the way you use the word, tell us. It is a useful conversation before a contract, not after one.
