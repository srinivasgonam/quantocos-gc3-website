    How to read this

    Capabilities are catalogs. Pods are the thing you buy.

    Every pod includes one or more of these catalogs, and the credit pool is spendable across whatever it includes. If you want a specific capability without a full pod, that is what the custom pods are for.

    The mapping

    Creative Pod → Creative catalog

    Strategy Pod → Creative + Growth + Strategy

    Capabilities, answered

    Four questions that come up before a pod is scoped.

    What the six catalogues are and how credits move between them.

    What are the six catalogues?

    Creative, digital marketing, strategy, interactive and XR, AI and automation, and back office. Every service in them carries its own credit value, dollar equivalent and turnaround in the published credit matrix.

    Are credits tied to one catalogue?

    No. A pod draws on one credit pool and can spend it across any of the catalogues it includes, so a month of content can become a month of automation without a new contract.

    How is a request priced before it starts?

    It is estimated in credits before work begins. Gate G1 is brief approval: the scope and the credit estimate are accepted before anything is produced.

    Can work be expedited?

    Yes. Priority is 1.5x credits with turnaround at half; rush is 2.0x credits at a quarter of the turnaround, capped at 20% of the monthly pool and dependent on capacity.
