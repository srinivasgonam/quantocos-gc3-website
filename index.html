    Questions buyers ask before the first call

    Answered from the published terms and the engagement letter, not from a sales deck.

    What is GC³?

    GC³ is Quantocos's Global Cloud Capability Center: a capability center you rent by the month instead of building one. Each pod pairs named specialists with AI agents and runs inside your own tools.

    What does a pod cost?

    Credits cost $25 each. Creative is $3,000 a month for 120 credits, Digital $6,000 for 240 and Strategy $9,000 for 360; Automation, Interactive and REMAU are scoped per client. No setup fee.

    How is this different from an agency, a freelancer or a GCC setup?

    An agency bills a project, a freelancer covers one discipline, and a GCC setup means staffing your own office in India. A pod is a monthly subscription to a working team, with published prices.

    How quickly does work start?

    Pods overlap your business day and work inside your tools from week one. Days 1–30 cover access, brand ingestion and the first brief.

    What are the five gates?

    G1 brief approval, G2 direction selection, G3 internal QA, G4 your review, G5 go-live signed by pod lead and client. G4 is your 48 business-hour review window, not a delivery promise.

    Who actually does the work, and where are they based?

    Named specialists work from India, overlapping your business day; some markets are served through independent delivery partners (for example Singapore).

    Which tools do you work in?

    Yours: Slack or Teams, ClickUp, Asana or Jira, Miro or Figma, plus the GC³ portal for briefs, estimates and approvals. We work in your accounts.

    What happens if I use more credits than my pod includes?

    The portal flags the shortfall before work starts: top up at $25 a credit, move the request to next month, or drop it. No surprise overage.

    How do I cancel?

    Give written notice before the next billing date, as set in the engagement letter. Work and source files created up to that date are handed over.

    Who owns the work?

    Copyright and IP assign to you, and work, source files and IP stay with you when the engagement ends.

    Is there a lock-in?

    Billed monthly. The minimum term and notice period are in the engagement letter before you sign.

    How are credits tracked?

    Balance and burn rate stay visible in the portal, drawn down as work moves through the gates.
