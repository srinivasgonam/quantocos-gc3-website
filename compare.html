    In short

    An offshore GCC is a captive team you employ; an agency sells projects or a retainer; a freelancer sells hours. A GC³ pod is a monthly subscription for a function, with a named manager, specialists, agents, tools and a visible credit pool. A pod is usually the lowest total cost when you need recurring work across several disciplines and do not want to hire.

    The real comparison

    What you actually pay

    A freelancer at $80 an hour looks cheaper than a $3,000 pod until you count the hours you spend briefing, chasing, reviewing and covering holidays. An agency retainer looks predictable until the scope changes. A captive team looks committed until the utilisation curve arrives.

    The pod is priced as a function, not as a person or a project. That is why the useful comparison is total cost of ownership: the fee, your management time, the tools, the cover and the cost of changing your mind.

    Three questions
    • How many hours a month will you spend managing this?
    • Who covers illness, holidays and a resignation?
    • What does it cost to stop, or to double the output for one month?

    When not to choose a pod

    The honest exceptions

    A pod is not the answer to every buying problem. If any of these is true, the other options in the table above are the better buy.

    One-off

    You need a single regulated opinion

    Legal, audit, clinical or tax sign-off belongs with a licensed professional firm, not a delivery pod.

    Narrow

    You need ten hours of one specialist

    A freelancer is the cheaper and more honest shape for a small, bounded task.

    Captive

    You want to own the team

    If the capability is core and you intend to employ it, a captive GCC gives you the control you are buying for.

    Scale

    You need fifty people next month

    A pod scales through credits and additional pods, not by becoming a recruitment program overnight.

    Questions

    What buyers ask before they compare

    Is a pod cheaper than an agency?

    For recurring work, usually yes. A Creative Pod is $3,000 a month for 120 credits, and a Digital Pod is $6,000 for 240 credits. An agency retainer for the same disciplines typically starts at $5,000–$20,000 a month, before media or production costs. A pod is not cheaper for a one-off project that fits inside a single quote.

    Is a pod the same as an offshore GCC?

    No. A global capability center is usually a captive team the client employs or contracts directly, with the client carrying recruitment, management and utilisation risk. A GC³ pod is a subscription: the team, the tools, the manager, the review gate and the credit pool are all included in one monthly figure.

    Can we start with one small piece of work?

    Yes. The first month can be a single catalog brief inside the pod's credit allowance. There is no setup fee, and the pod is month to month after the minimum term stated on the pod page.

    What happens to unused credits?

    Credits are a pool for the month and do not roll over. The allowance is agreed before the month starts, and the portal shows what has been spent and what is left.

    When is a pod the wrong choice?

    When you need a one-off regulated opinion, a single specialist for a few hours, or a 50-person transformation you intend to employ. In those cases a freelancer, a professional firm or a captive team is the honest answer.

    GC³ vs a GCC

    A capability center, or your own GCC?

    A Global Capability Center is an office you build, staff and run abroad. GC³ is a capability center you rent by the month instead of building one.

    Book a pod briefingSee pricing
    What each one is

    Two different things, honestly described

    Your own Global Capability Center

    An entity and an office in another market, with your processes, your headcount and your management overhead. It is built for scale you already have, and it takes months to stand up.

    • You hire, lease, comply and manage
    • Capacity is fixed once it is hired
    • The asset is yours, including the overhead

    GC³ pods

    A monthly subscription to a working team: one named manager, specialists and AI agents inside your tools. Published prices from $3,000 a month, and nothing is built on your side.

    • No entity, no office, no headcount of your own
    • One credit pool, estimated per brief
    • Month to month, with a stated notice period
