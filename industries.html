    Who we serve

    Four kinds of client, four different reasons

    We deliberately do not serve everyone. GC³ is built for foreign SMBs and mid-market businesses, the agencies that serve them, and property developers with a launch date.

    SegmentDigital Pod · Strategy Pod

    Foreign SMBs & mid-market

    You need a marketing department and cannot justify one. Hiring takes a quarter, agencies are inconsistent, and freelancers leave nothing behind when they go.

    A pod becomes the department: creative, media, landing pages and reporting, on a calendar you can plan around.

    Which pod covers this
    SegmentCreative Pod · Digital Pod · white-label

    Agencies & consultancies

    You have the client relationship and the strategy, but delivery capacity is the ceiling. Hiring to cover peaks is how agencies destroy their margin.

    Run a pod under your own brand. Capacity appears when the pitch is won and stops when the project ends.

    Which pod covers this
    SegmentREMAU

    Real estate & property developers

    A launch has a date that cannot move. Renders, walkthroughs, NRI demand campaigns and the CRM that catches the enquiries all have to arrive together.

    REMAU is a pod with one shape: project marketing, XR visualisation, diaspora targeting and pipeline automation on a launch clock.

    Which pod covers this
    SegmentDigital Pod · Interactive Pod

    Software & SaaS

    The product is good; the story is not landing. Documentation, onboarding experiences and demand all need to look like the same company.

    One pod holds positioning, demand generation and the interactive product experience — so the configurator and the campaign agree with each other.
