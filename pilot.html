    Terms

    What the pilot costs, and what happens after

    Pilot Sprint terms
    Length14 days
    Price$1,500
    Credits60, at $25 a credit
    Credit-backSubscribe to any pod within 14 days of the pilot ending and the $1,500 is credited against your first month.
    RefundUnused pilot credits are refunded.
    Lock-inNone — the pilot ends on its own.

    What you get

    • The same named pod manager as a full pod
    • The portal, with balance and burn rate
    • All five approval gates
    • NDA, data-processing agreement and IP assignment
    • Onboarding, brand ingestion, brief clarification and handover at no credit cost

    Who it is for

    • A team with one real piece of work and no appetite for a retainer
    • A buyer who wants to test the approval chain before subscribing
    • Not for a one-off logo, or every request you can think of at a flat fee
    How to start

    Three steps to day one

    01 — Send one brief

    Use the briefing form and mark it as a pilot. One real brief is enough for us to estimate it in credits.

    02 — Approve the estimate

    You see the credit estimate before work starts. If it does not fit 60 credits, we say so and scope it down.

    03 — Day one

    Day one is access and brand ingestion. The first brief runs through the five gates like any other work.
