    Institutional knowledge ends up in chat histories and private threads. The insight is real, but it is unreachable by anyone else, including the agents that are supposed to be helping.

    Sigma is a memory management tool, in development. It captures fragments worth keeping from any chat surface, compiles them into memory, turns them into skills and modules, and serves the result to agents and to Qore.

    A chat history records what was said. Sigma keeps what was learned, in a form another person or agent can reuse, with the fragments composited into modules rather than left as transcripts.

    In the tenant it belongs to, served through Apara, with role-based access control. Memory that cannot be permissioned is not much use to an enterprise.
